Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you sign up or use Inaya, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, hotel details, and any guest/booking data you enter into the system.

2. Location Information

Our guest-facing weather feature requests access to your device's precise location, with your permission, solely to display accurate local weather conditions during your stay. This location data is used only for this purpose, is not stored long-term, and is not shared with any third party except the weather data provider needed to fetch the forecast. You may deny location access at any time; the weather feature will simply be unavailable if you do.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to: provide and operate the Service, process payments, communicate with you about your account, and improve our platform.

4. Payment Information

Payments are processed securely through Cashfree Payments. We do not store your card, UPI, or banking details on our servers.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (MongoDB Atlas). Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the Service. If you close your hotel account, we retain data only as long as necessary for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate the Service, as listed below.

8. Third-Party Services We Use

Inaya relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy: Cashfree Payments (payment processing), Google Sign-In (optional login), a weather data provider (local weather display), and MongoDB Atlas (cloud database hosting). Our use of Google Sign-In data, where applicable, complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

9. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below.

10. Account & Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your hotel account and associated personal data at any time by emailing support@myhotelmanagementservice.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request." We will process verified deletion requests within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain records by law.

11. Children's Privacy

Inaya is intended for use by hotel businesses and their staff, and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Cookies

We use minimal cookies/local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences (such as currency and language).

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

14. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at support@myhotelmanagementservice.com.
