Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Free Trial

We offer a free trial plan so you can evaluate Inaya before making a purchase. We strongly encourage all customers to use the free trial prior to subscribing to a paid plan.

2. Digital Service — No General Refunds

Inaya is a digital service that is activated immediately upon successful payment. Because access is granted instantly, we do not offer refunds for change of mind, accidental purchase, or non-usage of the Service after payment.

3. How Payment Is Processed

All subscription purchases are processed securely through our website (myhotelmanagementservice.com) using our third-party payment processor. Payments are not processed through the Google Play Store billing system, and our Android application does not sell, unlock, or gate any digital content or subscription from within the app itself. If you install our Android application from the Google Play Store, it functions solely as a dashboard for hotel owners who already hold, or separately purchase, a valid subscription through our website.

4. Refunds for Service Issues

If you experience a technical issue that prevents you from accessing or using the Service, and the issue is caused by us, please contact our support team within 7 days of payment. We will work to resolve the issue promptly, and if we are unable to do so, we may issue a full or partial refund at our discretion.

5. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard or by contacting support. Cancellation stops future renewals but does not entitle you to a refund for the current billing period already paid.

6. Lifetime/Ownership Plans

One-time "Lifetime" or "Ownership" plans are non-refundable once the source code, credentials, or self-hosted access has been delivered, except in cases of a verified technical defect caused by us.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under the conditions above, email us at support@myhotelmanagementservice.com with your Hotel ID, payment date, and a description of the issue. We aim to respond within 3-5 business days.
